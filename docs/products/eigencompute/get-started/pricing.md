sidebar_position: 1

The EigenCompute testnet pricing is approximately 3.1X GCP for vCPU hour. 

Testnet pricing includes the following instance types: 

| Machine type     | vCPUs   |  Memory  | Architecture |
|------------------|:-------:|:--------:|---------------|
| g1-standard-4t   |    4    |  16 GB   | Intel TDX     |
| g1-standard-8t   |    8    |  32 GB   | Intel TDX     |

:::important
Current pricing is the EigenCompute testnet pricing. While in the Mainnet Alpha phase, the mainnet pricing is the same as
testnet pricing.
:::

Pricing is on-demand and you'll be prompted to provide billing details when deploying your first app to EigenCompute using the
[`eigenx app deploy` CLI command](../reference/cli-commands/deployment.md).
